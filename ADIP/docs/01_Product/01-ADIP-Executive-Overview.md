# 01 — ADIP Executive Overview

## Vision

**ADIP — AI Delivery Intelligence Platform** is the executive operating cockpit for an
AI-driven SDLC. It transforms a multi-portfolio, multi-domain banking technology
estate into a single, AI-orchestrated control surface for the CIO, CTO, CRO and CISO.

ADIP is positioned not as a KPI dashboard, but as an **AI Executive Delivery
Intelligence Platform** — a platform that *thinks*, *recommends* and *generates*
on behalf of the executive, while preserving auditability and governance.

## Objectives

1. **AI-first SDLC** — every SDLC phase (Requirements, Architecture, Development,
   Testing, Release, Audit) is fronted by an AI Copilot that finds, recommends
   and generates artifacts from the current evidence.
2. **Single Source of Truth** — one canonical place for AI Governance, AI
   Evaluation, AI Observability and the **Universal Artifact Repository**.
3. **Executive Decision Orientation** — every center answers four questions
   within 30 seconds: *what does this domain own*, *what is its current health*,
   *what are the top risks*, *what does AI recommend*.
4. **Governed Generation** — every AI-generated artifact is captured in the
   Universal Artifacts Repository, version-tracked, status-tagged and
   downloadable for board review or audit.
5. **Demonstrable ROI** — a documented value model with formulas, KPI
   traceability and a payback calculator that an executive can interrogate.

## Business Value

| Lever | Outcome | Indicative Impact |
