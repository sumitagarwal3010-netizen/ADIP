# 09 — Executive Demo Talking Points (CIO Script)

A condensed CIO-facing script that frames the same demo path in business
language. Use this when speaking to a board / executive audience.

## Opening — 60 seconds

> "We've moved beyond AI-as-a-feature. ADIP is our **AI Delivery Intelligence
> Platform** — a single executive control surface for our entire SDLC,
> governance, risk and value realization estate. Everything you see is live;
> the platform ticks every 30 seconds with correlated banking telemetry.
> When I generate an artifact in this demo, it is captured in a governed
> repository — versioned, status-tagged and downloadable for board
> consumption."

## Key talking points by act

### 1. Executive Control Tower
- "Four KPIs answer four CIO-level questions: *Can we deliver?* *Is the
  estate healthy?* *Where could we fail?* *What value are we realizing?*"
- "I can generate a narrative executive summary on demand — the platform
  consumes the live state and writes the brief I would otherwise pay
  consultants to draft."

### 2. AI SDLC Copilot
- "Our AI Copilots don't just measure — they **analyze, find, recommend
  and author**. Watch."
- *(Generate a Requirement Review Report)* "That artifact is now in our
  governed repository. It has an executive summary, findings,
  recommendations, an approval status and a downloadable body."
- "We've eliminated dashboards-as-busywork. The Copilots write the
  documents that used to take days."

### 3. AI Governance Single Source of Truth
- "We faced two big risks last year: *AI sprawl* and *governance debt*. We
  fixed both. There is now exactly **one** AI Risk Register, **one** model
  inventory, **one** prompt registry, **one** controls library. Every other
  surface is a rollup or a deep link."
- "Risk & Compliance shows the *summary* of AI risk — but if you want the
  register itself, it's a one-click deep link to AI Governance."

### 4. Portfolio AI Advisor
- "Our portfolio is bigger than any human can mentally model. The AI
  Advisor classifies every active investment into **Accelerate, Stop, Fund,
  At-Risk** with quantified business impact. That is decisional output, not
  reporting."

### 5. Risk, Architecture, Technology
- "Each governance center now owns *only* what it owns. No cross-domain
  KPI inflation, no duplicate tabs, no overlapping reports."
- "Every center supports artifact generation — auditable, downloadable
  evidence on demand."

### 6. Universal Artifact Repository
- "Here is everything we've generated in the last 15 minutes. Searchable,
  filterable, downloadable. This is the demonstrable proof that AI is doing
  the work — not just rendering it."

### 7. KPI Catalog
- "Every KPI on the platform is documented — definition, formula, source,
  frequency, owner, executive consumer. We can audit any number we present
  in 5 seconds. This is what *KPI governance* looks like."

## Closing — 30 seconds

> "ADIP is not a dashboard. It is an **AI executive operating system** —
> with governed AI generation, traceable KPIs and audit-grade artifacts —
> for the CIO, CTO, CRO and CISO."

## Anticipated Questions

| Q | A |
| --- | --- |
| "Where do these numbers come from?" | KPI Catalog → search the metric → see formula, source, owner. |
| "Where does an artifact go after generation?" | Universal Artifact Repository → versioned, tagged, downloadable. |
| "How do you avoid AI sprawl?" | Single source of truth: AI Governance owns risk/controls/models/prompts; AI Evaluation owns quality; AI Observability owns runtime. |
| "What if I disagree with a Copilot recommendation?" | Every artifact has an Approval Status — you reject it; the recommendation is audited and re-run. |
| "Can I run this against my real data?" | The `simulationEngine` is replaceable; production wiring connects to your engineering signals (Jira, GitHub, ServiceNow, etc.). |
