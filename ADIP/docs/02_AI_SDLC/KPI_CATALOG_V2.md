## 0. Executive Summary of the Redesign

The redesign moved the platform from a **single simulation-driven executive dashboard** (4
"health" KPIs sourced from one `state.executive` object) to a **modular, engine-driven
executive control tower** built on four strategic pillars, each backed by its own data engine
and drilldown surface.

| Aspect | Previous | Current |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Executive headline KPIs | Delivery / Production / Governance / Engineering Health | Delivery Health / Technology Health / Risk Posture / Value Realized |
| KPI data source | One `mockDataEngine.js` `state.executive` object | Four dedicated engines (`copilotEngine`, `technologyStrategyEngine`, `enterpriseRiskEngine`, `valueRealizationEngine`) |
| Navigation | Flat hub list | 7 hierarchical groups → sections → leaves |
| Drilldown keying | Label-keyed (`'Delivery Health'`) | chartId-keyed (`'copilot.delivery-health'`) |
| AI governance at exec level | Not surfaced | "Enterprise AI Health" rollup (5 metrics) |

## 2. EXECUTIVE KPIs (Tier 1 — Control Tower Headline)

Source: `src/pages/ExecutiveControlTower.tsx` lines 46–57.

### 2.1 Delivery Health
- **Definition:** Average delivery health across all AI Delivery Copilot projects.
- **Formula:** `round(Σ project.healthScore / projectCount)` — `computeCopilotKpis()` in `src/data/copilotEngine.ts` (L28–30).
- **Source:** `COPILOT_PROJECTS` (`copilotMockData.ts`).
- **Unit:** % · **chartId:** `copilot.delivery-health` · **Trend shown:** +2.4%
- **Owner:** CIO / Head of Delivery.
- **Thresholds (suggested):** ≥85 healthy · 70–84 watch · <70 at risk.
- **Executive interpretation:** "Is the AI-assisted delivery portfolio on track?"

### 2.2 Technology Health
- **Definition:** Share of the technology estate on strategic/preferred/approved lifecycle.
- **Formula:** `round(strategicCount / TECHNOLOGIES.length * 100)` — `computeTechnologyStrategyKpis()` (`technologyStrategyEngine.ts` L22–23).
- **Source:** `TECHNOLOGIES` (`technologyStrategyMock.ts`).
- **Unit:** % · **chartId:** `technology-strategy.technology-health` · **Trend:** +1.8%
- **Owner:** CTO / Chief Architect.
- **Thresholds:** ≥80 healthy · 60–79 watch · <60 elevated debt.
- **Executive interpretation:** "Is our technology estate modern and standards-aligned?"

### 2.3 Risk Posture
- **Definition:** Mean inherent risk exposure across the enterprise risk register.
- **Formula:** `round(Σ risk.inherentScore / riskCount)` — `computeEnterpriseRiskKpis()` (`enterpriseRiskEngine.ts` L22).
- **Source:** `ERM_ENTERPRISE_RISKS` (`enterpriseRiskMock.ts`).
- **Unit:** /100 (lower is better) · **chartId:** `enterprise-risk.enterprise-risk-exposure` · **Trend:** −3.1% (improving)
- **Owner:** CRO.
- **Thresholds:** ≤40 controlled · 41–60 elevated · >60 critical.
- **Executive interpretation:** "What is our aggregate enterprise risk exposure?"

### 2.4 Value Realized
- **Definition:** Annual value realized across value-tracked projects.
- **Formula:** `Σ project.valueRealized`, displayed as `₹(annualValue / 1,000,000)M` — `computeValueRealizationKpis()` (`valueRealizationEngine.ts` L27, L48).
- **Source:** `VALUE_PROJECTS` (`valueRealizationMock.ts`).
- **Unit:** ₹M · **chartId:** `value-realization.annual-value` · **Trend:** = `valueKpis.roi`
- **Owner:** CFO / Transformation Office.
- **Thresholds:** ROI ≥100% strong · 40–99% moderate · <40% review.
- **Executive interpretation:** "What financial value has the program delivered?"

## 9. Thresholds & Interpretation Convention
- **Percent health KPIs** (Delivery/Technology/Portfolio/Transformation Health, coverage, adoption): ≥80 good · 60–79 watch · <60 act.
- **Risk KPIs** (Risk Posture, Residual, Cyber, AI, Audit risk, exposure): lower is better; ≤40 controlled · 41–60 elevated · >60 critical.
- **Financial KPIs** (Value Realized, ROI, Cost Avoidance): trend + ROI ≥100% strong.
- **Count KPIs** (Open Findings, Open Risks, Incidents, Breaches): zero-target; any non-zero needs an owner + due date.
